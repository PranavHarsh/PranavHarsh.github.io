Selected work

ParkEase

Smart urban parking · Live on Vercel & Render
  • Drivers find, reserve and pay for parking in real time, with live spot availability, dynamic pricing and QR-based gate access.
  • Location-aware search with a “near me” mode, an adjustable radius and amenity filters for covered bays, security, EV charging and bike racks.
  • Role-based sign-up splits the product in two: Drivers book spots, Operators manage lots and set their own pricing.
  • Built and deployed solo — a Next.js front end on Vercel, an Express, Prisma and Socket.io API on Render, and a managed PostgreSQL database on Neon.
